I'm Samuel Okoosi — a First-Class Statistics graduate from the University of Ibadan (CGPA: 3.63/4.0) with a deep focus on data analysis, machine learning, and statistical modeling.

My work sits at the intersection of quantitative rigor and real-world impact. I've built ML models for churn prediction, loan default analysis, and price forecasting; led field data collection and time-series economic analysis at UI-LISA; and contributed to research on oil price shocks and inflation in Nigeria using ARFIMA and fractional integration methods.

Beyond the technical work, I believe data scientists must communicate well. I've tutored Statistics and Mathematics for 3+ years, hosted academic seminars, and spoken publicly on excellence and leadership.
