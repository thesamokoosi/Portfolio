I turn messy, real-world data into decisions — the full pipeline from raw numbers and feature engineering to predictive models, NLP pipelines, and research-grade time series analysis. My work spans predictive modelling for churn, loan default, and price forecasting; NLP and sentiment analysis on unstructured text; and economic research into oil price shocks, fractional integration, and inflation dynamics in Nigeria.

Before the models, there's fieldwork: I led a five-person team at UI-LISA tracking commodity price trends across Ibadan's markets, week over week, by hand. That grounding in how data actually gets collected shapes how I model it.

I also think data scientists have to explain themselves clearly. I've tutored eight-plus statistics and mathematics courses over three years, organised academic seminars, and written for student communities — because a model nobody understands doesn't change anything.
